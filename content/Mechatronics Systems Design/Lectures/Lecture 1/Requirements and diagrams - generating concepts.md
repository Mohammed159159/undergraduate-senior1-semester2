# Requirements
- Need -> Objective -> Requirements proposal -> Technical requirements
- Include safety requirements
- In general, categorize the requirements (ergonomic, *safety*, functional, etc.)
- Qualitative (marketing requirements)
	- Needs hierarchy
- Quantitative (engineering/performance requirements)
	- Design requirements table
- Bonus: assign weights for the requirements to evaluate different solutions
