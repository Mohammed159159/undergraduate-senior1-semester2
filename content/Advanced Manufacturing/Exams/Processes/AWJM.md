## Applications and Products
**Applications**
- *Industries*: automotive, aerospace, electronics
- *Materials*: aluminum, titanium, heat resistant alloys, granite
**Products**
- Engine components for aircrafts
- Titanium body parts for aircrafts
- Aluminum body parts for automobiles

## Advantages and Disadvantages
- *Similar to [[WJM#Advantages and Disadvantages|WJM Advantages and Disadvantages]]* 
- It is like [[AJM]] + [[WJM]]

**Advantages**
- Best of both processes: can cut both hard and soft materials or in very thick sections
	- Low kerf even with 200mm-thick materials
- Low cutting forces on WP
- Localized structural changes
- No slag/cutting losses
- Limited tooling requirements
- Finish 125-250 microns

**Disadvantages**
- High noise levels
- Cannot cut materials sensitive to moisture (degrade quickly)
- Kerf width (1.5-2.3mm) varies with material thickness
		- ==Hard materials -> widest kerf at the top==
		- ==Soft materials -> widest kerf at the bottom==
	- Taper on cut edge is a function of material hardness

## Working Principle
- [[AJM]] + [[WJM]]
- Just like [[WJM]], however abrasives are mixed with the water jet
- Specialist for hard materials
