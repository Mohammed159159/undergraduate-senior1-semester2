## Turning 
- Collet, 3 jaw chuck
	- Faster
	- Better gripping
	- Lower Eccentricity
- Long WP
- Manual operations -> motors CNC
- Lower quantity
- R&D -> manual | CNC -> mass production 
## Milling
- In-feed
- CNC is more common
- HSS, carbide, carbide tip
- Drilling needs less accurate tolerance, flexible tool, using end mill could lead to oval shapes or eccentric holes
## Drilling
- Interference, sleeve - 3-jaw drill chuck
- Drilling -> moving bed -> milling
## Shaper
- Heavy duty and large scale
- Initial
- Quick
